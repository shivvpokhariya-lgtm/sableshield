    Why this looks safe

      Important — how it works

      AI watches the conversation, not the number.

      Live, not after

      The transcript appears line by line, and every scam line is flagged as it is spoken — before money moves, not after.

      Private by design

      Open-weight Gemma 4 runs on the device. Audio never leaves the phone — no recording, no storage, and you opt in.

      Signals it catches

      OTP demands, “blocked in 2 hours” urgency, “do not tell anyone” isolation, and spoofed caller ID — the patterns behind almost every scam call.

      Safe calls pass too

      A legit call is not just cleared — the demo shows exactly why it is safe, so you can trust the verdict both ways.

      Live mode — try it yourself

      Paste a real call. Get a verdict.

      Test your own words

      Paste any caller line and the engine scores it live — the same verdict, on words the demo didn't script.

      Gemma 4 with a free key

      Add a free Gemini API key and the transcript is analysed live by Gemma 4.

      Zero fail without one

      No key? Instant rule-based analysis still runs — OTP, urgency, isolation, and pressure patterns scored on the spot.

      You confirm every action

      The app never spends money or sends messages on its own — Hang Up and Report always need your tap.

      Free key: aistudio.google.com/apikey · Without a key, instant rule-based analysis runs (zero fail). · A key you enter is saved in this browser and loads automatically next time.
